1 LFA1 — Vendor Master (General Data)

LFA1 is the central SAP table for vendor master general data. It stores client-independent information about vendors — details that apply across all company codes, such as name, address, tax numbers, and communication data. Every vendor in SAP has exactly one record in LFA1, identified by the vendor number (LIFNR).

3 Table Relationships

The vendor master is split across three main tables following SAP's layered architecture:

Key Insight

LFA1 is client-independent (one record per vendor). LFB1 extends this with company-code-specific data (payment terms, reconciliation account). LFC1 stores cumulative transaction figures per fiscal year for AP reporting.
